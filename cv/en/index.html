Profile

Bachelor of International Trade and Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Marketing (CETYS Universidad, 2017), with 21 years of experience in the maritime and port industry. For 12 years he was a federal public servant at the Integral Port Administration of Ensenada (now ASIPONA Ensenada), where from 2012 to 2022 he compiled the official port statistics of Ensenada, El Sauzal and Costa Azul: more than 162 official documents.

He was Deputy Manager of Operations and Ecology, and founder and general coordinator of the 1st National Symposium on Customs-Bonded Facilities operated by the Port Administrations (API) (2013). In 2014, with a scholarship from Puertos del Estado (Spain) and the OAS Inter-American Committee on Ports (CIP), he completed in Santander the 14th Ibero-American Course on Technology, Operations and Environmental Management in Ports (University of Cantabria), the origin of the project for Mexico's first green port.

He taught at the undergraduate level from 2013 to 2017. Since 2018 he has directed ContenedorEns and Arsa Bodegas, companies dedicated to the sale, rental and modification of containers, and since 2024 he has been an independent port advisor.

Foreign trade, customs-bonded facility and sanitary inspection

  • Foreign trade and customs (2005–2009): operational coordinator of the clearance of goods at the port of Ensenada at Agencia Aduanal Soriano y Rivera: import and export customs procedures, as well as international transits.
  • Customs-bonded facility (API Ensenada): in charge of the customs-bonded facility and the cabotage areas, as well as their storage control. As part of the port's reorganisation, he managed the request to modify the facility's area, which went from about 29,000–31,000 m² to 21,694 m² (2.17 ha), to give up space for the new fiscal access lane to the Customs facilities. From 2009 he amended the storage application and form with disclaimer clauses for natural or unforeseeable damage; that clause later served to defend the Mexican party in a multi-million-dollar lawsuit.
  • Zoosanitary and phytosanitary inspection points (2013–2022): as head of the customs-bonded facility, he filed the first application and obtained the authorisations for the first international zoosanitary and phytosanitary inspection point at the seaport of Ensenada, installed inside the customs-bonded facility (2013). He then processed the renewal of both authorisations every two years until 2022 and handled the audits of SENASICA's Agricultural Health Inspection Office (OISA).
  • API Ensenada Transport Regulation Centre (2009–2022): in the Operations Department he took part, from its construction (September 2009 to July 2011), in the interdepartmental management of the first Transport Regulation Centre operated by API Ensenada, in all its stages: planning, infrastructure works, contracting and start-up. As a member of its committee, he was involved in the daily handling and operation of freight-truck access to the ports. The centre, located 500 m from Federal Highway No. 3 Ensenada–Tecate and with 103 bays, regulates the flow of vehicles bound for the ports of Ensenada and El Sauzal, gives carriers a space to prepare their trips and performs security checks under the ISPS Code and mechanical inspections to prevent oil spills (Administración Portuaria Integral de Ensenada [API Ensenada], 2015). Its operation reduced truck parking on city streets and helped prevent security risks and road accidents.
  • Source: API Ensenada (2015; 2016, pp. 36–37).
  • Secondary navigation channel for small vessels, port of Ensenada: as part of the Operations Department team, he took part in the project that made Ensenada the first port with a buoy-marked secondary navigation channel for small tourism, artisanal fishing and sport-fishing vessels. This second lane separates their traffic from the main channel used by ocean-going ships, improving the safety of inner navigation. He was involved in the planning, installation, execution, start-up and review of the channel, which remains in operation.
  • Renewal of the ports' Operating Rules (Ensenada and Costa Azul; El Sauzal): within the Operations Committee and as second-in-charge of the Operations Department, under the coordination of his direct supervisor, the project leader, he took part in the renewal of both instruments: the Operating Rules of the Port of Ensenada and Costa Azul, and those of the Port of El Sauzal. He was responsible for editing, analysing and verifying versions; managing and delivering official letters; and presenting and explaining the changes in detail to the committees. The process required reviewing a large volume of operational and regulatory information and incorporating participants' comments into the versions. When he left in 2022 the procedure was still in progress; the following administration obtained final approval, and the new rules for both ports are in force.
  • Source: Administración del Sistema Portuario Nacional Ensenada (n.d.).
  • Customs Facilitation Committee of the General Customs Administration (AGA), based at the Ensenada Customs Office (11-0): representative of API Ensenada.
  • 2017 earthquake emergency: loading, inventory, safekeeping and dispatch of containers with relief supplies to Oaxaca.

Short sea shipping and the Ensenada–San Diego ferry

In 2014, on returning from the Santander diploma, he proposed to the Directorate General for Port Development and Administration (CGPMM) that Short Sea Shipping be applied in Ensenada, as one of 3 projects. Baja California authorities estimate that it could begin operations in December 2026 (SanDiegoRed, 2026).

Intertopía©, neologism and conceptual proposalFebruary 2026Submitted to the RAE (under study for the 24th edition of the DLE); registered with Safe Creative (2602144563892)

Intertopía© (concept): an intermediate space arising from the tension between utopia and dystopia, understood not as irreconcilable opposites but as two possibilities that inhabit the human mind. In that space, action and the repetition of thought lead human beings to question themselves and to generate a dynamic balance; thus intertopia becomes a field of potentiality in which human action, guided by reflection, can transform the conditions of the future without imposing an absolute judgement on good or evil, letting freedom and being define their course. The author sent the proposal on 13 February 2026 to the Interactive Dictionary Unit of the Royal Spanish Academy (RAE), which acknowledged receipt the same day and stated that it will be studied and assessed for possible inclusion in the 24th edition of the Diccionario de la lengua española. The text was registered with Safe Creative on 14 February 2026 (2602144563892).
