Professional summary

Port consultant with 21 years of experience in the maritime and port industry (since 2005), including 12 years as a federal public servant at the Ensenada Port Authority (API, now ASIPONA Ensenada, Mexico). Specialist in official port statistics, customs-bonded facility (recinto fiscalizado) operations, customs and sanitary audits, and container logistics. Container-industry entrepreneur since 2018 and doctoral candidate in Business (CETYS Universidad), researching the statistical, fiscal and sanitary registration of second-use shipping containers in Mexico.

Key qualifications

  • Official port statistics: produced the official statistics of the ports of Ensenada, El Sauzal and Costa Azul for Mexico's General Coordination of Ports and Merchant Marine, 2012–2022 (more than 162 official documents).
  • Customs-bonded facilities and trade facilitation: managed the port's customs-bonded facility; handled audits by Ensenada Customs, the Tax Administration Service (SAT) and SENASICA (animal and plant health); founded and coordinated the 1st National Symposium on Customs-Bonded Facilities of Mexican Port Authorities (2013, 7 port authorities).
  • Sanitary inspection: led (2015–2022) the creation of the first international animal and plant health inspection point at the Port of Ensenada.
  • Regulation and quality: contributor to the Port Operating Rules of Ensenada and Costa Azul; ISO 9001 / 14001 internal auditor for 4 years.
  • Green ports: collaborated on Mexico's first green port project (2023 Maritime Award of the Americas, OAS–CIP, granted to ASIPONA Ensenada).
  • Capacity building: undergraduate lecturer (2013–2017); certified trainer under Mexico's CONOCER standard EC0217.
